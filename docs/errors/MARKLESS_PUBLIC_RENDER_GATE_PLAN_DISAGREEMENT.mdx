**Error** from the compiler, while it builds your `.tsrx` files.

## What happened

Markless prints the message, the reason, and a suggestion:

```text
MARKLESS_PUBLIC_RENDER_GATE_PLAN_DISAGREEMENT: <label> passed render support checks but has no usable render plan
The public render gate admitted this authored construct, so silently omitting its render plan would produce output that can fail or remain permanently unsettled at request time.
https://markless.dev/errors/MARKLESS_PUBLIC_RENDER_GATE_PLAN_DISAGREEMENT
```

Words in angle brackets change from case to case.
